# ⚖️ Legal RAG Assistant

Explore Indian Supreme Court precedents through grounded, source-cited answers.

## What it does

- Searches judgments using hybrid BM25 and semantic retrieval
- Reranks the most relevant passages
- Generates answers with Google Gemini
- Displays case details and source excerpts for verification

## Try an example

- Summarize the legal principles in a Supreme Court judgment.
- Find precedents on constitutional rights or due process.
- Compare cases involving similar legal issues.

> This tool is for educational legal research and does not replace advice from a qualified professional.
